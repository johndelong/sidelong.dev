Buying an industrial trash compactor usually means phone calls, still photos, data sheets and brochures. For a customer who knows nothing about compactors, that's confusing. They have to work out what they need from a pile of model numbers, without any real picture of what will end up behind their building.

A friend of mine installs compactors, and he gets those calls. Compactor Spec is what we built together to make them easier: a configurator that teaches you what you need for your situation as you go.

It starts with your building, not a catalog. You choose where the compactor will go, and from there you only see equipment that works in that spot. If you know what you want, you can build the package yourself. If you don't, it asks one practical question at a time and recommends something. Every choice appears on a 3D model of the site, so you can see what you'd get and how it would be installed before anyone comes out.

When you're done, the configuration becomes a build sheet you can send off for a quote. On the other side is an admin portal, where my friend sees analytics, quote requests and help inquiries, and can configure the entire configurator himself, down to the 3D models and where they sit. It's live now and ready to take quote requests.
