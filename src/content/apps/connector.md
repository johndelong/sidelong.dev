On a work trip to Jacksonville, Pete, Ryan and I were throwing around app ideas. One of them was a cocktail recipe app where you could actually watch the drink come together in the glass. It sounded like a blast to make together.

We named it after a room at Michigan Software Labs that connected the two partners’ offices. We’d end up there to celebrate or just catch up and share what was going on in our lives. I enjoy a cocktail now and then, and what I remember most about that room is the fellowship. Building Connector gave the three of us another reason to spend time together, and we got a useful little app out of it.

Pete was the idea guy. Ryan helped design the app and make the artwork. I did a lot of the coding and drew the glasses in Figma. One detail I had fun with was the way the liquid waves in the glass. Once we had the ingredients drawn as layers, you could see how much of the drink each one made up. Getting to make it move was part of the fun.

I’d love to come back to it someday. I’ve found recipes in AI chats that I’d happily make again, if I could remember which chat they were in. I’d like Connector to give those recipes a home and help me figure out what I can make with what I already have. And I’d happily spend another evening making the liquid wave just right. The recipe would be fine either way.
