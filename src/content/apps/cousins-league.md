Grandma wanted to get the grandkids into fantasy football. We drew names from a hat for the draft order and took turns over FaceTime picking four NFL teams each. Five kids play, and Grandma and Grandpa are in it too.

Cousins League is where I go to see how everybody’s teams did and where the cousins stand. It pulls in live NFL scores and keeps the league standings up to date. The standings tell me who’s ahead, but I’m usually just as curious about who had the best week or whose team pulled off a comeback. That’s why I added weekly awards, streaks and league records. It gives us more to talk about when we check in.

The first version was a little program that made printable scorecards, with each player’s teams and matchups filled in. The girls still work out some of the scores with me. If we miss a week or somebody wants a second opinion on the math, I can pull up the app and catch us up. I built much of it by typing to [Rhys](/apps/rhys/), our household assistant, from the road.

Grandma and Grandpa bought trophies for first and last place, to be handed on the following year. The last-place trophy says “you stink.” We’ve been careful to explain that this is a judgment of the picks.

I also built a season outlook to see whether I could simulate the rest of the schedule. I’m still deciding whether to show that one to the kids. There’s plenty of fun left in the season, even if a chart thinks otherwise. I’d like the app to grow with our league and maybe open it up to other families someday.
